## What is Phantom?

Phantom is a **sovereign digital organism** — a decentralized, offline‑first network for sealing, sharing, and remembering human thought.

- **Seal an idea** — cryptographically prove that you wrote something at a specific moment.
- **Sync with other nodes** — exchange sealed thoughts over Bluetooth, LoRa, local WiFi, or even Tor (via `.onion`).
- **Keep a private diary** — long‑form entries, encrypted on your device, with optional local AI reflection (Ollama).
- **Relay messages** — act as a blind carrier, forwarding seals between nodes that never meet directly.
- **Build reputation** — the Suijuris ledger records contributions (seals, relays, encounters) without a central authority.
- **Protect your data** — everything is encrypted at rest (AES‑256‑GCM) and authenticated with Ed25519 signatures.

Phantom is built for the real world:

- Works offline (no internet required).
- Runs on Android (via Termux) and desktop (Windows / Linux / Mac).
- No account, no servers, no cloud — you are the network.

Why does it exist?

There is a woman in Lagos. She has a secondhand Android phone. She sends money to her mother every month and pays 8% in fees because she has no alternative. She learned to self-censor without anyone asking her to.

Phantom exists for her.

Not as a metaphor. As a design criterion.

If she can't use it — it's not Phantom.

Principles that never change

    Privacy by architecture — not by policy.

    No central authority — no one owns it.

    Universal access — no one is excluded for lack of money.

    Radical transparency — all decisions are public.

    Founders become obsolete by design — Phantom must survive without them.

    The organism evolves — the principles do not.

    Phantom exists for human beings — not for itself.

The Lagos Protocol

Before any major decision, ask:

    Can she use it?
    (A woman in Lagos with a secondhand Android phone.)

    Does it actually protect her?
    (Real protection, not apparent.)

    Does it change something concrete in her life?
    (Not in theory. In practice.)

    Can this be used against her by someone close to her?
    (The gap that was named late, and must always be asked.)

How to contribute

You don't need to be a developer.

Build code: Read CONTRIBUTING.md. The architecture is documented. The tests pass.
